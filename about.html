About us

Who we are and how we work

Genesis Eduworld is an education and skill development organization in Jaipur, working to strengthen the overall capabilities of students and young people.

Our story

Started in 2020, during the pandemic

Genesis Eduworld was established in 2020 during the Covid-19 pandemic. Since then it has worked to build the overall capabilities of students and young people through structured training, workshops, mentoring and personality development initiatives.

Whether sessions were online during the pandemic or offline, the organization says it has kept its quality standards high and stayed committed to giving students the best possible learning opportunities.

The aim is to develop the competencies that help students perform well not only in academics, but also in higher education, employment and everyday life.

Our mission is to identify individual potential, nurture talent, and equip every learner with the skills required for personal growth and professional success.

Dr. Lokesh Gupta, Director

Our approach

Learning, skills and practice, together

Potential needs the right setting

Every student has potential. Genesis Eduworld believes it takes the right environment, guidance, confidence and skills to turn that potential into achievement.

Learn by doing

The approach is practical and student-centred. Participants are encouraged to learn through participation, practice, interaction and real-life situations.

Knowledge plus skills

Education is not only about acquiring knowledge. It is about practical skills, confidence, communication, leadership and a positive attitude, so students can meet the challenges of today’s competitive world.

What we hope to see

Young people who are skilled, confident and future-ready

Genesis Eduworld believes India’s youth development needs coordinated effort from educational institutions, government agencies, trainers and the community. It hopes to contribute to a generation of students who are:

  • Skilled
  • Confident
  • Responsible
  • Employable
  • Future-Ready

It also welcomes the chance to collaborate with educational institutions and the Government on youth empowerment.
